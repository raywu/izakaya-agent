# Japanese authenticity checks

These checks apply to every item promoted into trusted scenario content.

## Required review fields

Verify:
- speaker role: staff / customer / either;
- learn-as status: recognition / production / both;
- communicative function;
- canonical Japanese;
- reading / romanization;
- intended context;
- pragmatic boundary;
- safe variation;
- forbidden generation;
- validation status.

## Guardrails

Reject or flag:
- staff honorific/service language taught as customer production;
- literal English translations that are grammatical but unnatural in context;
- unnecessarily formal or stiff customer Japanese;
- textbook forms presented as the dominant real-world form without evidence;
- invented etiquette or universal cultural claims;
- unvalidated \"more natural\" corrections;
- ad hoc romaji spellings;
- recognition variants generated only for variety;
- a phrase marked native-validated without actual qualified native-speaker review.

## Native-review questions

For the compact MVP fixture, ask a qualified native Japanese reviewer:

1. Would someone actually say/hear this in this context?
2. Is the speaker role correct?
3. Is the register appropriate?
4. Is there a simpler/more common beginner production form?
5. Would a customer sound odd saying the production target?
6. Which staff variants materially matter for recognition?
7. Are the pragmatic notes accurate but appropriately bounded?
8. Are the readings/romaji correct?
9. Does the proposed entry -> seating -> first-drink flow represent a plausible common path?

Agent review is not native review. Keep `native review pending` until this has happened.
