## Scenario goal

Enter a common izakaya interaction, state party size, follow the seating cue, and order one basic drink.

This is one common path, not a universal sequence. Real restaurants may ask about reservations, seating, ordering systems, or other details.

## Romanization

Use standard Hepburn romanization with macrons for learner-facing romaji.

## Communicative functions

### welcome

**Role:** staff  
**Learn as:** recognition  
**Function:** recognize the standard restaurant welcome  
**Canonical:** いらっしゃいませ  
**Reading:** irasshaimase  
**Context:** staff greeting on entry  
**Pragmatics:** a customer usually does not need to answer verbally; do not teach this as a universal rule about greetings  
**Safe variation:** scene context may vary; keep the Japanese form trusted  
**Do not generate:** learner production that imitates staff service language  
**Validation:** research-validated; native review pending

### ask_party_size

**Role:** staff  
**Learn as:** recognition  
**Function:** ask how many people are in the party  
**Canonical:** 何名様でしょうか？

## MVP generation boundaries

The runtime tutor may freely vary:
- whether the learner enters alone or with a friend;
- which validated drink the learner chooses;
- English scene narration;
- timing and amount of scaffolding;
- whether a trusted recognition variant is used after the anchor is stable.

The runtime tutor must not invent learner-facing Japanese and then present it as:
- the preferred production form;
- a naturalness correction;
- a canonical staff expression;
- a trusted recognition variant.

If required Japanese is missing, restructure around trusted content or flag the missing function for content validation.

## Sources / provenance

The entry and ordering staff functions above are grounded in Japanese restaurant-industry materials that explicitly list expressions such as 「いらっしゃいませ」「何名様でしょうか」「こちらへどうぞ」「ご注文はお決まりですか」 as restaurant service language.

Contemporary izakaya listings also confirm common drink categories/items including 生ビール, ハイボール, and ウーロン茶.

The customer production forms and repair phrase remain **native-review pending** even where they are widely understandable. The MVP must not label them native-validated until a qualified native speaker reviews the fixture.


## World-response boundary

No post-order staff acknowledgement is currently trusted in this fixture.

After a successful drink order, the runtime tutor should advance with concise English/nonverbal narration (for example, that the drink is on its way) rather than inventing Japanese staff acknowledgement.

Candidate acknowledgement language must go through the same research/native-review workflow before being added here.
