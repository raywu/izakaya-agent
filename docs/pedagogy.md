# Pedagogy

Izakaya Agent is task-first: the learner acquires Japanese because it is needed to progress through a real situation.

## Core principles

- **Situation is the curriculum spine.** Grammar and vocabulary are introduced just in time.
- **Recognition breadth, production depth.** Learners need to understand more natural staff language than they need to produce.
- **Retrieval over exposure.** A safe first success is followed by reduced support and a later retrieval attempt.
- **Repeat functions, not scripts.** Change context, item, trusted wording, or modality to test transfer.
- **Correct without derailing communication.** Brief support, retry, return to the scene.
- **Repair is a skill.** Learners should eventually know how to recover when they do not understand.
- **Modality evidence stays honest.** Reading a transcript is not listening; successful transcription is not pronunciation quality.
- **Culture is behavioral.** Teach what the learner needs to do appropriately, with narrow contextual claims.

The product is intentionally not organized around JLPT levels, a grammar sequence, or translation drills.

The runtime rules live in `AGENTS.md`; this document is rationale only.
